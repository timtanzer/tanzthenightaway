What this is

This is a personal set of notes on how to approach interviews for public hospital roles in Queensland, especially early-career to mid-level positions within medicines services. It focuses on preparation habits and answer structures that tend to hold up across different teams and sites.

It is designed to be practical. You can skim it quickly, or use it like a workbook and build your own set of examples as you go.

What this is not

  • It is not an official resource.
  • It is not endorsed by any hospital, service, or government organisation.
  • It is not a promise of outcomes, and it will not match every panel or every site.

Treat it as a starting point, then adjust based on the role, the local context, and your own strengths.

How to use this guide

  1. Start with the position description and extract the key themes.
  2. Choose a small set of strengths you can prove with real examples.
  3. Build a short example bank you can adapt to different question wording.
  4. Practise answering out loud and tighten your timing.
  5. On the day, listen for the theme, then choose the best-fit example and structure it clearly.

Quick prep checklist

  • I can describe what the role is trying to achieve in one sentence.
  • I have 6 to 10 examples written as dot points (not scripts).
  • Each example includes: the risk, my action, and the outcome.
  • I can explain how I prioritise when everything feels urgent.
  • I have two thoughtful questions to ask the panel at the end.

Note: These pages use plain language on purpose. If you prefer a more formal style, you can rewrite any section in your own voice.
