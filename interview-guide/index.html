This is a personal set of notes on how to approach interviews for public hospital roles in Queensland, especially early-career to mid-level health practitioner positions. It focuses on preparation and answer structures that tend to hold up across different teams and sites.

It is designed to be practical. It is not a comprehensive guide to all possible questions, but it should give you a good foundation to build on.

  • It is not an official resource.
  • It is not endorsed by any hospital, service, or government organisation.
  • It is not a promise of outcomes, and it will not match every panel or every site.
